## Selected analytics projects

### 01 · [Customer Behavior & Business Performance Analysis](https://github.com/thepranishpradhan/customer_behavior_analysis)
**Python · Pandas · SQL · PostgreSQL · Power BI**

**Business focus:** understand purchasing patterns, customer segments, product ratings, and revenue across **3,900 shopping records**.

- Cleaned and prepared the data, wrote SQL queries addressing **10 business questions**, and built an interactive Power BI dashboard.
- The repository reports nearly equal average spending for subscribers and non-subscribers ($59.49 vs. $59.87), illustrating why total revenue alone can be misleading.
- Demonstrates data preparation, aggregate queries, CTEs, window functions, customer segmentation, and KPI communication.

[Read the case study](https://github.com/thepranishpradhan/customer_behavior_analysis) · [Python](https://github.com/thepranishpradhan/customer_behavior_analysis/tree/main/notebooks) · [SQL](https://github.com/thepranishpradhan/customer_behavior_analysis/tree/main/sql) · [Dashboard](https://github.com/thepranishpradhan/customer_behavior_analysis/tree/main/dashboard) · [Report](https://github.com/thepranishpradhan/customer_behavior_analysis/tree/main/report)

### 02 · [NYC 311 HPD Complaint Resolution Analysis](https://github.com/thepranishpradhan/nyc311-hpd-complaint-analysis)
**Python · PostgreSQL · Power BI · Business requirements · BPMN**

**Business focus:** examine complaint-resolution time and develop a prioritization proposal using a bounded sample of **405 closed tickets**.

- Connects data cleaning and **five SQL analyses** with a business requirements document, current-state process map, recommendation memo, and Power BI dashboard.
- Uses median and percentile measures, a 30-day analytical threshold, and a sensitivity check for **37 duplicate-flagged rows**.
- Demonstrates how analytical findings can inform a documented proposal while keeping assumptions and sample limitations visible.

*Independent portfolio exercise using public data from narrow extraction windows on April 1-3, 2024. It is not representative of all HPD complaints or an official HPD engagement; the proposed pilot is not an implemented outcome.*

[Read the case study](https://github.com/thepranishpradhan/nyc311-hpd-complaint-analysis) · [Requirements & memo](https://github.com/thepranishpradhan/nyc311-hpd-complaint-analysis/tree/main/docs) · [SQL](https://github.com/thepranishpradhan/nyc311-hpd-complaint-analysis/tree/main/sql) · [Process map](https://github.com/thepranishpradhan/nyc311-hpd-complaint-analysis/tree/main/process%20map) · [Dashboard](https://github.com/thepranishpradhan/nyc311-hpd-complaint-analysis/tree/main/dashboard)
